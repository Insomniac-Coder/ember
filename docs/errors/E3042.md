# E3042 — retired: a part handed over, then its owner used whole

`E3042` is no longer emitted. It refused using a value whole after one of its parts was handed over on its own, before the part was given a new value.

Since 0.9.20 it is `E3040`: "`pair.first` was handed over to `_first` on line 16; `pair` is used whole on line 18; give it a new value first, or keep a copy: `_first = copy of pair.first`".
