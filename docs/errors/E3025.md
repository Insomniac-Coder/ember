# E3025 — retired: a method takes all of `this`

`E3025` is no longer emitted. It refused a call of a method while another name held part of its object, on the reading that a method takes all of `this`.

Since 0.9.20 what every method changes and reads is worked out from its body (`[BRW-10]`), so no method claims all of its object. A call conflicts only with what its body touches: `p.set_x(10)` while `a = ref mut p.x` is used changes `p.x` through two names (`E3022`), and a call of a method that touches only `p.y` is accepted.
