The three unsure points, settled by the lead (the safe reading):
- **U1** a suspended generator across a reload of its body: the reload plan refuses a body change that
  changes a live generator frame's layout or states.
- **U2** `@reinit_on_reload` on a static that another static's link may point into: refused in the plan.
- **U3** a `thread_shared` object's `drop` may run on any thread that holds a count of it, a view's count
  included, so the changes its `drop` makes count on that thread (E7012 where they reach a main-thread
  `static mut`).
